# 04. CRM and data operations

## The setup

- **HubSpot** is the CRM of record.
- **Apollo** runs sequencing, people sourcing and enrichment.
- **ZoomInfo** adds people sourcing where Apollo is thin.
- **Lusha** covers small companies under 50 staff.
- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** is for account research and personal outreach.

Cold outreach is kept out of HubSpot's marketing email on purpose, which protects the health of the main domain.

## Daily discipline

- **Reconciliation ledger.** From 7 Sep to 22 Sep I reconciled every send, reply and bounce against the plan, day by day.
- **Suppression list.** Anyone who should not be followed up is labelled once and excluded everywhere.
- **Mailbox allocation.** Each contact is tied to one sending mailbox, so a prospect always hears from the same address.
- **Two numbers, kept apart.** Apollo reports unique contacts reached per sequence. Total messages sent is a different figure. I track both so neither is mistaken for the other.

## Enrichment routine that saves credits

1. Search companies first.
2. Search contacts only on the companies that qualify, senior roles only.
3. Enrich emails. Phone numbers cost about five times as much, so they are pulled only when a call is planned.

## Reporting

- A weekly pipeline review built on five measures: pipeline value, qualified meetings, proposals issued, deals closed and revenue collected.
- A daily sales tracker in Google Sheets with HubSpot updated the same day.
- A one-step qualification check for any new prospect, so fit is judged the same way every time.
