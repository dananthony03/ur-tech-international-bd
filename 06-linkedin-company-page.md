## LinkedIn outreach plan

On 29 Aug 2026 I built a LinkedIn pack to run alongside email, with every message written and ready to send.

**Three tiers**

1. **New reach.** Senior buyers who could not be reached by email get an InMail.
2. **Double-tap.** Buyers already in an email sequence get one InMail on the same topic, because a second channel lifts response.
3. **Channel switch.** When an email sequence finishes, one InMail closes the loop properly.

**Rules I wrote for it**

- InMails under 100 words, no links, no attachments
- Connection notes under 200 characters
- 15 to 20 connection requests a day at most
- Every message asks an honest question and makes it easy to say no
- A reply on any channel stops every other touch to that person

**Daily loop, 15 minutes**

15 connection requests, 5 InMails, then clear the acceptances.

**Budget**

145 InMail credits planned across the three tiers, with about 50 held in reserve.
