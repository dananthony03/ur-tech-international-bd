# 03. Government tender track

## The rule

Government bodies are never cold-emailed. This track runs only through official portals, supplier registrations and bids. The government target list is a bid-research asset and nothing else.

## What I built

A master source, verified on 24 Aug 2026, covering four regions: the UK, Europe, Australia and New Zealand, and the UAE. For each one it sets out the portal, the registration steps, the documents to have ready and the best route in.

| Region | Portals | Route in |
|---|---|---|
| United Kingdom | Find a Tender (Central Digital Platform), Contracts Finder | One Login, organisation registration and PPON, supplier profile and Share Code, saved alerts for cloud, software, web, managed services and BPO. Crown Commercial Service frameworks such as G-Cloud and Digital Outcomes as the next step. |
| Europe | TED, then each member state's own portal (PLACE and BOAMP in France, DTVP in Germany, ANAC in Italy, TenderNed in the Netherlands) | EU Login, CPV-code alerts for IT, software and web services, national registration as opportunities appear |
| Australia | AusTender, BuyICT, and state portals (buy.nsw, VendorPanel, QTenders) | Free registration and category alerts, per-tender lodgement, BuyICT marketplace panels |
| New Zealand | GETS | Supplier registration, alerts for ICT, digital and software, standing invitations and panels |
| UAE | Ministry of Finance Digital Procurement Platform and Federal Supplier Register | Federal supplier registration, then tender access |

## Opportunities assessed

Each opportunity got a clear call: go, assess, register and apply, or pass.

- GovCMS Drupal panel, Australia: flagship
- Health NZ DISI prequalification, New Zealand
- DIA Payroll and ERP, New Zealand
- NZ Government Marketplace

Panels and standing invitations were ranked first because they stay open for months or years and are the lowest-cost way into repeat public-sector work.

## Bid readiness

I also listed what a strong bid needs on file, so it can be gathered once and reused: company registration details, recent accounts, verified case studies, and Cyber Essentials for UK buyers.
