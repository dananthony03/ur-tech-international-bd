## The short version

I started on Gmail, writing cold emails by hand, and moved the whole motion into Apollo. Each sequence was written for one role in one market and opened on a real, dated fact about the prospect. Alongside that I defined a two-tier customer profile, sized every market before choosing it, built a government tender route that runs only through portals and bids, set up HubSpot as the CRM of record, rebuilt the LinkedIn company page, researched the website against 100 competitors, and wrote the revenue plan I presented to the Founder and CEO.
