# 07. Website and competitor research

## Why I did it

A cold email sends the buyer to the website. So the site is part of the sales motion, and I treated it that way.

## Site review

I went through urtechcomputers.com page by page, including the sitemap, the service pages, the product pages and the mobile view, and presented the findings to the Founder and CEO and the head of IT on 28 Sep 2026.

## 100-competitor benchmark

I studied 100 competitors and found one pattern in what the strongest of them show a buyer. Seven things:

1. Reviews on Clutch or G2
2. Named case studies
3. Price bands
4. The real team
5. Industries served
6. Certifications, with evidence
7. A working path to a demo

That list became the checklist for the new site.

## Nine reference sites

On 4 Oct 2026 I completed a full study of nine sites in the same space, each with its own record of structure, page flow and copy:

EverHelp, Kensium, Itransition, Invedus, Syberry, Brocoders, WOW24-7, Helplama, SunTec India

## New website proposal

The research feeds one proposal: a single new website for UR Tech, delivered to the leadership as a deck of page creatives with the copy written for each page. The site is planned around the lines the company leads with, so a buyer sees straight away what UR Tech does. The technical build stays with the in-house engineering team.
