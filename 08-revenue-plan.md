# 08. Revenue plan

On 30 Sep 2026 I presented a revenue plan to the Founder and CEO: one owner for the company's international revenue, with a 30/60/90-day plan, a year-one plan and a three-year view.

## What the plan leads with

- AI-enabled custom development and ERP, new build or upgrade
- KPO/BPO managed services
- Kuzhu HRMS
- Web, app and marketing, bundled with ERP

## 30/60/90

| | Day 30 | Day 60 | Day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outreach | 8 mailboxes warmed, 3 sequences live (ERP, KPO/BPO, Kuzhu), 200 emails a day by Day 21 | 200 a day, steady. ERP bundled with web, app and support | Playbook written down so an SDR can repeat it |
| Calls and proposals | 10 calls, 4 proposals | 20 calls, 8 proposals | 30 calls, 12+ proposals |
| Tenders | Company papers in | Cyber Essentials done. UK, NZ and EU portals registered | First submissions filed |
| Website | Improvement list delivered in week one | Improvements live | Case studies live |

## Three-year view

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Team | Hands-on, one owner | Two SDRs added | Five-person revenue team |
| Growth comes from | ERP, KPO/BPO, Kuzhu and the bundle | BPO at scale, cloud attached to every ERP | Partner and reseller channel, an AI-connectivity layer across ERP, HRMS and BPO |
| Tenders | UK pipeline open | G-Cloud and NHS frameworks | Established bid function |

## How the engine runs

**Daily.** 200 researched emails across the mailboxes, 10 new accounts researched, replies answered within 2 hours, HubSpot updated the same day.

**Weekly.** 10 calls, 2 proposals, and a 30-minute Monday review with the CEO.

**Monthly.** Five KPIs reported, a deliverability audit, website and case-study updates.

**Quarterly.** A new market or service line, tender submissions, a pricing review.

## The market behind it

- ERP software: $106,000,000,000 in 2026, growing to $282,000,000,000 by 2034, about 13% a year
- BPO: $354,000,000,000 in 2026, growing to $696,000,000,000 by 2033, about 9.9% a year

Source: Fortune Business Insights.

## Stack and cost discipline

The plan runs on a lean stack: Apollo, HubSpot, Sales Navigator, Zapier and email verification, with every paid tool justified by a stated purpose and a date it is needed from. No ad spend.
